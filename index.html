About Me

A strategist focused on revenue, not just reach

Here's a quick look at who I am and how I've helped e-commerce brands grow through email.

I'm an e-commerce email marketing strategist with over 2 years of experience helping US and EU direct-to-consumer brands build email programs that actually drive revenue — not just open rates. My focus is on the full lifecycle: campaign strategy, flow automation, template design, and the ongoing testing that turns a good email program into a brand's most reliable sales channel.

I've partnered with brands across skincare, home goods, and fashion, rebuilding abandoned cart and post-purchase flows, redesigning template systems for better deliverability, and running segmentation strategies that consistently lift conversion and repeat purchase rate.
